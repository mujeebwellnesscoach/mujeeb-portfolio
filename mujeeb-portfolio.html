I am Mahammed Mujeeb, the founder of MD Nutrition Centre and a passionate wellness coach dedicated to helping individuals reclaim their health. With over five years of hands-on experience, I have guided hundreds of clients through transformative journeys — from weight management and diabetes care to improving everyday energy and vitality.

My approach is deeply personalised. I believe no two bodies are the same, and every client deserves a plan that respects their lifestyle, cultural food habits, and personal goals. Whether you are from Guntur or anywhere across Andhra Pradesh, I design programmes that fit your life — not the other way around.

At MD Nutrition Centre, we go beyond calorie counting. We build sustainable habits, educate on the science of nutrition, and support our clients every step of the way — through WhatsApp, in-person sessions, and dedicated follow-ups. Our mission: Nourish Your Body. Transform Your Life.
